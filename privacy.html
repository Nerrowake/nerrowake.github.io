Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nerrowake collects, uses, and protects information related to Wakebot, the official Nerrowake Discord bot.

Information We Collect

Wakebot may collect and store limited Discord-related information needed to operate community features. This may include Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, command usage, moderation records, warning records, configuration settings, and leveling or experience data when those features are enabled.

Information We Do Not Collect

Wakebot does not collect Discord passwords, payment information, private direct messages, or sensitive personal information. Wakebot does not sell user data.

How We Use Information

Information is used to provide bot functionality, including moderation, server configuration, role management, welcome messages, announcements, leveling features, playtester tools, and other Nerrowake community features.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent collected data. Information may be shared only when required to operate the bot, comply with legal obligations, protect the community, or respond to abuse, security, or moderation concerns.

Data Retention

Wakebot retains information only as long as needed to provide its features, maintain server records, resolve moderation issues, or comply with applicable requirements. Server administrators may request removal of stored server configuration data.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect stored information. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we work to keep Wakebot's data limited and protected.

Children's Privacy

Wakebot is intended for use within Discord communities and is not directed toward children under the age required by Discord's own terms and policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Wakebot evolves. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact Nerrowake at: nerrowake@gmail.com

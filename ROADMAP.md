# Roadmap

This roadmap tracks planned improvements to the Nerrowake legal site.

## Now

- Keep Wakebot privacy policy and terms of service available through GitHub
  Pages.
- Preserve a simple static site with no build step.

## Next

- Add a clear changelog entry whenever legal policy text changes.
- Add review notes for policy updates.

## Later

- Add additional product-specific legal pages when new Nerrowake tools need
  them.
